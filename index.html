Where do these prices come from?

Premiums and drug lists update from CMS. Annual pharmacy estimates come from the Medicare printout you import, or amounts you enter. Imports are dated snapshots and do not refresh themselves.

Use the same prescriptions, quantities, location, assistance status, and pharmacies in Medicare Plan Finder ↗. Import a new printout when those details or prices change.
