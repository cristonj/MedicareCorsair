Where do these prices come from?

Premiums and drug lists update from CMS. Annual pharmacy estimates come from Medicare results you collect or import, or amounts you enter. Imports are dated snapshots and do not refresh themselves.

Use the same prescriptions, quantities, location, assistance status, and pharmacies in Medicare Plan Finder ↗. Collect new results when those details or prices change.
