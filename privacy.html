Privacy

Operated by Criston Jackson · jacksoncris44@gmail.com

Your information

Your coverage, medication list, pharmacies, providers, and comparison estimates are held in memory while you use the app. The app does not save profiles or results to browser storage or the helper's database. Closing the page and the desktop helper clears their working memory. A saved profile from an earlier version is removed when this page starts.

The helper stores public Medicare plan data on your computer to support automatic updates. Your medication search text is sent to the U.S. National Library of Medicine to find matches. Information entered on Medicare.gov is handled by Medicare.gov.

Imported results

Medicare results are transferred directly to the dashboard without extension storage. PDF pages are processed locally in memory, including image recognition. Any PDFs you created yourself remain wherever you saved them, under your control.

Your browser, operating system, Medicare.gov, and any files you choose to create have their own storage behavior. The app cannot control those. Contact us without including sensitive details if you have privacy questions.
