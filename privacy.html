Privacy Notice

Effective October 29, 2026 · Version 2026-10-29.1

MedicareCorsair is operated by Criston Jackson, jacksoncris44@gmail.com. This notice describes the current local service. An internet-hosted version or new integrations may require a revised notice before launch.

Information used

The tool uses the coverage type, current plan names, priorities, ZIP code/state, pharmacy/provider preferences, help-with-costs answers, medication products, quantities, days supply, and optional annual estimates you enter. Coverage setup and provider preferences stay in this browser; the comparison request sends only the state and medications to the local service. No account, Medicare number, Social Security number, date of birth, exact street address, or payment information is required. Do not enter unnecessary identifiers.

Where it goes

Medication search text is sent through this app’s service to the U.S. National Library of Medicine’s RxNorm service to find medication names. NLM receives the search text and the service’s network address; NLM’s own privacy practices apply. NLM search results and search text may be cached in the service’s memory for up to 24 hours, or until the cache is cleared by capacity limits or a restart. Cached entries are not associated with a user identity.

Your state and medication list are sent to this app’s comparison service and matched against a locally stored public CMS dataset. The app does not persist your comparison request or medication list in its server database or application access logs. Public CMS data is refreshed independently of your medication searches. The current server binds to your computer’s loopback address and is intended for local use.

Browser storage

Your working list and estimates are held in browser memory during use. If you select “Save on this device,” the list, state, and entered estimates are stored in this browser’s local storage until you clear them, turn off that setting, or clear browser data. Other people using the same browser profile may be able to access saved information.

Sharing, ads, and external links

The current app has no advertising trackers, third-party analytics, sale of personal information, or commission-based plan placement. Links open outside services only when you choose them. The app does not automatically send your full medication list to Medicare Plan Finder, SHIP, or an insurer. Information you enter on those services is subject to their policies. Ordinary network operations by external providers may create records under those providers’ own practices.

Imported documents

If you upload a Medicare printout or saved page, it is sent to this local service for extraction. PDF rendering and OCR run locally; documents and page images are not sent to an external OCR provider. Temporary rendered images are deleted after extraction. The uploaded document and extracted full text are not saved by the service. Reviewed plan estimates are held in browser memory, or in local storage if you choose to save on this device. Saved HTML is read as text; scripts are not executed. The current service processes documents on this computer. An internet-hosted version would need a revised notice.

Optional browser extension

When you click Collect all result pages on Medicare.gov, the extension reads the visible results and follows the results pagination. It does not read account credentials, browser cookies, or network authorization headers. The collected page text can include plan prices, location, and provider information displayed in that comparison. Results are stored in extension session storage until replaced by a new collection or the browser session ends. Clicking Send to MedicareCorsair transfers them to the local dashboard for review and local parsing. The extension has access to the active tab you invoke it on and the local dashboard URLs; it does not run on unrelated websites. Downloaded collection files remain under your control.

Your choices

Use the tool without saving your list. Choose “Clear” to remove the working list and its saved copy from this browser. Downloaded checklists, printed pages, and PDFs are under your control and must be removed separately. To stop future requests, close the page; to stop the local service, stop its server process.

Security and contact

The service minimizes collection and blocks cross-origin access to comparison actions. No technology guarantees absolute security. Browser storage and downloaded files are not encrypted by this app. Avoid saving health information on a shared device. This notice does not represent that the tool is HIPAA certified or suitable for handling clinical records.

Contact jacksoncris44@gmail.com with privacy questions. Do not include medication lists, identity documents, or other sensitive details in an initial email.
